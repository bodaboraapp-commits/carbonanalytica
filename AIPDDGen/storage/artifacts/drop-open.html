TECHNOLOGY DISTRIBUTION

From first prospect to a technology that keeps working

We run the full distribution chain for clean-cooking and energy technologies — finding the right households, placing units, securing carbon rights and keeping every unit in service, with data captured at each step.

01

Prospecting

Identify and map the households, communities, retailers and local partners where the technology fits demand and current fuel use.

02

Qualification

Screen each prospect before a unit is placed — fuel use, existing stove, location and willingness to use it — so the baseline is documented from day one.

03

Distribution

Deliver technology to qualified households through field teams and local partners, with user training, GPS-tagged handover and unit registration.

04

Carbon waiver

Collect signed user consent to waive or assign carbon rights, linked to the unit and household record, so emission reductions can be claimed once and traced back to the source.

05

Technology maintenance

Follow-up visits, repairs, replacements and usage checks that keep units in service and keep ongoing usage data ready for MRV and reporting.

TRACK RECORD

Evidence built from the field

Ongoing applied research with a leading East African cookstove manufacturer, running since 2019.

2019 — PRESENT

Kitchen Performance Testing — biomass & electric cookstoves

Multi-day household monitoring across 300–500 households per cycle in urban and peri-urban Kenya, tracking fuel consumption, stove-stacking, and satisfaction against traditional stove baselines. Supports ongoing carbon credit MRV documentation.

2019 — PRESENT

Customer surveys & focus groups — multi-product line

Quantitative and qualitative market research across 200–400 households per cycle, spanning wood, charcoal, and electric product lines. Findings shaped go-to-market strategy and product refinement.

2019 — PRESENT

Controlled Cooking Tests with emissions monitoring

Lab-partnered CCTs measuring fuel use, cook time, and PM2.5/CO emissions against baseline, consistent with IWA/ISO protocols and independently reported emissions reductions exceeding 60%.
